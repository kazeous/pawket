# Overdue refunds

1. Treat any overdue or attention-required obligation as a critical liability incident. Freeze unrelated creator review if it competes for the owner.
2. Verify the authoritative PostgreSQL due date and locked destination; do not derive the deadline from email, queue, or Valkey.
3. With fresh owner TOTP, send or resolve the refund manually and record the outcome. Pawket must not move funds automatically.
4. If blocked by bank/provider access, preserve the liability, set the fixed attention outcome, document the external incident reference, and retry through the bank’s approved channel.
5. Confirm audit, refund, outbox, email disposition, and alert recovery. Notify the applicant without bank details or internal notes.
6. Close only after accounting evidence and a second review show no outstanding or unmatched liability remains.
