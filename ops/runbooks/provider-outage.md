# Provider outage

Identify the provider first; preserve PostgreSQL as the authority.

- SMTP: committed business state remains valid and delivery is at least once. A provider may accept a message before Pawket persists `sent_at`, so a lost acknowledgement or crash can produce a duplicate safe notice. Do not infer that a retry means the first send was rejected. Restore the sender and use the domain state plus the stable handoff ID, `sent_at`, attempt count, and attention status as authoritative evidence. Sender exceptions remain retryable for attempts one and two; the third unknown outcome becomes terminal `attention_required`, clears destination/secret envelopes, and keeps `sent_at` unset. If attempt three crashes, hangs, or fails during decryption, its next job invocation atomically gives the same terminal disposition after the lease expires, without decrypting or contacting SMTP. During rollout preflight, identify unsent nonterminal rows with `attempts >= 3`; do not replay or rewrite them manually, because the compatible worker recovers expired/no-lease rows while preserving an active unexpired owner. Existing terminal replays are acknowledged without incrementing the attention metric again. That terminal state does not prove rejection: inspect provider-side evidence using the stable handoff ID and incident window, then follow a separately approved remediation. Never replay sent or unknown-outcome handoffs blindly. Keep the three-attempt bound coupled to the system queue's eight exponential attempts and the handoff's 60-second availability delay; review all three settings together.
- authentik: new login and step-up fail closed. An existing Pawket session works only within its bounded five-minute IdP lease. After expiry perform a top-level silent check; no refresh token or background polling extends the lease. Public pages stay available. Restore the configured provider and verify email, primary/TOTP evidence, issuer/client and flow revision. Do not enable the retired password/social runtime as a fallback.
- authentik SMTP: distinguish a successful send task/SMTP acknowledgement from inbox delivery and a signed callback. Check the stage's global/custom SMTP selection, server and worker configuration, provider rejection/bounce evidence and recipient inbox/spam. Never bypass verified-email policy. Pawket's worker retires queued legacy credential emails as `auth_moved`; business notices retain their normal retry behavior.
- PostgreSQL: web and worker readiness must fail. Stop mutations and recover the database before queue work.
- Valkey: outbox rows remain authoritative. Restore Valkey/worker, then dispatch using outbox IDs as job IDs; do not reconstruct events from logs.

After recovery, verify worker poll timestamps, backlog ages, failed jobs, email dispositions, refund deadlines, and revision attestation. Record provider incident ID and UTC window without credentials or PII.
